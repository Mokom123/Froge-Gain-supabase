Privacy Policy

Froge Gains ("we", "us", "the app"). Last updated: 16 November 2026.

This policy explains what information the Froge Gains app collects, why, and the choices you have. By creating an account you agree to it.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: email address, username, display name, profile photo, password (stored securely by our authentication provider, never readable by us), and your country if you give it.
  • Social accounts you link: your handles on X, Telegram, YouTube, TikTok and Facebook, so we can check that you completed tasks. For YouTube we use Google sign-in with read-only access to check likes, comments and subscriptions, and nothing else.
  • Wallet address: if you connect a wallet, we store its public address. The app never holds your funds, private keys or seed phrase.
  • Identity verification (optional): if you choose to verify your identity, the details and documents you submit.
  • Activity in the app: tasks completed, FGP earned and spent, daily claims and streaks, referrals, game bets and results, posts, comments, reactions, statuses, group and private messages, and photos, videos or voice notes you send.
  • Device information: a push-notification token so we can send alerts, and basic technical data such as app version and error logs.

2. How we use it

  • To run your account, verify tasks and credit FGP.
  • To power features: leaderboards, referrals, chats, groups, feed, statuses and games.
  • To send notifications you allow (tasks, follows, messages, game turns).
  • To prevent fraud, abuse and duplicate accounts, and to keep the community safe.
  • To improve and support the app, and to meet legal duties.

3. What other people can see

Your username, display name, profile photo, badge, posts, statuses, leaderboard position and public profile details can be seen by other users. Private messages are visible only to the people in the chat. Photos, videos and voice notes in chats are deleted from our storage after 14 days. Statuses are deleted 24 hours after they are posted.

4. Google user data

Froge Gains uses Google user data only to check that you liked, commented on or subscribed to the YouTube content in a task you chose to complete. The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell this data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties. You can disconnect your Google account in the app or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We use trusted service providers to run the app: Supabase (database, authentication and file storage), Google Firebase (push notifications), and, when you use them, X, Telegram, Google/YouTube and your wallet provider. They process data only to provide their service. We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect users and the app.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is active. Chat media and statuses are deleted on the schedule above. If you delete your account we delete or anonymise your personal data, except where we must keep records for legal or fraud-prevention reasons.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and protected by access rules in our database. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and never share it or your wallet seed phrase.

8. Your choices and rights

  • You can edit your profile, unlink social accounts, turn notifications off and block users inside the app.
  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, or to delete your account, by contacting our support team.

9. Children

Froge Gains is not for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has an account, contact us and we will remove it.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and we will tell you in the app about important changes.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: our support team.
